# Phase 10 – Athom-compliant device icon refinement

The Homey driver/device icon at `drivers/solar_controller/assets/icon.svg` was refined to follow Athom's driver icon guidance:

- 960×960 SVG canvas
- transparent background
- monochrome line art
- no gradients or background shapes
- no large filled silhouette
- device uses most of the canvas for good small-size recognition
- distinct from the app icon

No runtime functionality was changed.
